RESEARCH AT THE INTERSECTION OF TECHNOLOGY AND SOCIETY

Computer science,
social systems, and the questions between them.

I'm Selah Frederickson, an interdisciplinary student and researcher interested in using computational methods to better understand how people interact with data, technology, and intelligent systems, especially in educational and social contexts. Drawing on both computer science and sociology, I approach research questions from both computational and social perspectives.
