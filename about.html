A LITTLE ABOUT ME AND MY RESEARCH

An academic weapon,
an aspiring researcher, and a creator of beautiful things.

I'm Selah Frederickson, an interdisciplinary student at the University of Montana majoring in Computer Science and Sociology with minors in Mathematics and English. I am interested in researching and learning more about how computational methods can help us understand the systems people create, the technologies they use, and the societies they shape.

MY ACADEMIC BACKGROUND

Four Interconnected Areas of Study

01

Computer Science

I study computational methods, algorithms, and software development. I'm especially interested in using technical tools to investigate complex problems and make information more accessible.

02

Sociology

Sociology helps me ask how people, institutions, and social structures shape one another. It encourages me to consider the human context behind the data and systems I study.

03

Mathematics

Mathematics strengthens my quantitative reasoning and provides vital background information for the algorithmic and data-driven solutions that my research focuses on.

04

English

My studies in English inform how I read, write, and communicate complex ideas. I complete my research with the public in mind: all people should be able to interact with and benefit from my findings.
